ProLocker – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

01 Introduction

Welcome to ProLocker ("App", "we", "us", "our"). We are dedicated to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy details how ProLocker handles your information when you use our app locking, call blocking, backup, file vault, and utility services.

By using ProLocker, you consent to the data practices described in this document.

On-Device Security Guarantee: All your sensitive data—including lock credentials, contacts backup files, hidden vault files, and call blocking rules—remains strictly on your device. We do not upload your personal files, contacts, or communications to our servers.
02 Information We Collect & Process

2.1 Stored Exclusively on Your Device

  • Lock Credentials: Encrypted PIN codes, lock patterns, and recovery security questions.
  • Contacts Backup Data: Contact lists exported or restored solely during user-initiated backup actions. Stored in standard VCF or local backup files on your device storage.
  • File Vault (Hidden Files): Photos, videos, audios, and documents protected inside the private vault.
  • Call Blocking Rules: Phone numbers and blacklist rules configured by you for spam and unwanted call rejection.
  • Intruder Selfies: Photos taken locally when an unauthorized person attempts to unlock protected apps with an incorrect password.

2.2 On-Device Data Processing

  • Usage Statistics (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Evaluated locally in real-time to detect when a locked app opens so the lock screen appears immediately.
  • Installed Packages (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): Queried locally to list apps for locking and APK backup extraction.
  • Call Screening & Phone State: Incoming phone numbers are matched against your local blacklist in real-time to filter unwanted calls via Android Call Screening and Phone State APIs. No call audio or call history is recorded or uploaded.
  • Junk Cleaning: Scans device storage locally to identify temporary files, cache, and obsolete APKs to free up space.

2.3 Automatically Collected by Third-Party SDKs

  • Advertising Identifiers (AD_ID): Used by ad networks to deliver relevant advertisements and measure ad performance.
  • Device Telemetry & Network Info: Basic device model and OS version used for configuration delivery and crash prevention.
  • Push Notification Token: Firebase Cloud Messaging token used exclusively to deliver important security and app updates.
03 Permissions Explained

ProLocker only requests permissions strictly required for its core features:

Permission Purpose
PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS Detect when a locked app is brought to the foreground to present the lock screen.
QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES Enumerate installed applications for app locking and APK backup extraction.
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW Display the lock screen overlay securely on top of locked apps.
Accessibility Service Ensure timely lock screen display across all Android versions.
READ_CONTACTS / WRITE_CONTACTS Export and restore contact backups locally when initiated by the user.
READ_PHONE_STATE / ANSWER_PHONE_CALLS Enable Call Blocker to identify incoming caller numbers against your blacklist and filter spam calls.
CAMERA Capture photos of intruders entering incorrect passwords (optional feature).
MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / STORAGE Manage File Vault items, save local backup files, and run the Junk Cleaner scan.
USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT Support fingerprint and biometric unlocking.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS / FOREGROUND_SERVICE Keep continuous background lock and call screening protection active.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Restart security services automatically after device reboot.
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS Prevent Android battery savers from disabling lock and call screening services.
INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Fetch remote configuration and serve advertisements.
AD_ID Advertising identifiers for ad delivery.
VIBRATE Provide haptic feedback on pattern and PIN input.

05 Data Security & Retention

We implement strict data protection measures:

  • All sensitive data (passwords, contacts backups, hidden vault files) is stored locally on your device in secure storage.
  • Network communications (e.g. remote configuration) use HTTPS encryption.
  • Uninstalling the App or clearing app data removes all App-generated configuration and stored credentials. Backup files in your chosen external folders remain under your personal control.
06 Children's Privacy

ProLocker is not designed for or targeted to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

07 User Control & Rights

You have full control over your privacy:

  • Grant or revoke permissions (Contacts, Storage, Camera, Phone) at any time through Android System Settings.
  • Export, delete, or relocate your local backup files as you choose.
  • Opt out of interest-based ads via Android Google Ads settings.
