ProLocker – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2025  |  Last Updated: September 2, 2026

01 Introduction

Welcome to ProLocker. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights when you use the ProLocker application ("App", "Service").

ProLocker is an advanced security application that extends beyond standard app locking to include contact backup, SMS management, and call blocking features. Because of these additional capabilities, this policy addresses certain data categories more thoroughly than standard locker apps.

Our Core Principle: All sensitive personal data (contacts, call logs, SMS, lock credentials) is stored exclusively on your device. We do not upload your personal communications data to any server.
02 Information We Collect

2.1 Information Stored Locally (Never Sent to Our Servers)

  • Your PIN codes, lock patterns, and recovery questions/answers
  • List of apps you choose to lock
  • App settings and preferences
  • Contact data accessed for the Contacts Backup feature (stored locally or exported by you)
  • Call log data accessed for the Call Log Backup and Call Blocker features
  • SMS data read for the SMS Backup feature (read-only, stored locally)
  • Blocked caller lists and call blocking rules

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Statistics: App usage data (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) to detect when a locked app is opened — processed on-device only.
  • Installed Packages: List of installed apps (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES) to display lockable apps — local only.
  • Phone State: Call state information (READ_PHONE_STATE) to enable call blocking. We detect incoming call numbers to match against your blocked list — no call data is transmitted.
  • Device Advertising ID: Used by advertising partners to serve relevant ads (see Section 4).
  • Network & Connection Info: Basic connectivity for remote config and ads.

2.3 Sensitive Data Handling

⚠️ Contacts, Call Logs, and SMS: ProLocker accesses these only when you explicitly use the Backup or Call Blocker features. This data is:
  • Read directly from your device's local database
  • Never uploaded to our servers
  • Stored only in the backup files you create on your own device or storage
  • Used for call blocking matching exclusively on-device in real-time

2.4 Camera (Optional Feature)

ProLocker may access the camera to capture an intruder photo when a wrong PIN is entered, if you enable this optional feature. Photos are stored locally on your device only.

2.5 Firebase Cloud Messaging

Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) is used for push notifications (security alerts, app updates). An installation token is assigned by Firebase but is not used for any tracking beyond notification delivery.

03 Permissions Explained

ProLocker requests the following permissions. Rows marked with a purple background are additional permissions beyond those in AppLocker:

Permission Purpose
INTERNET Load advertisements and remote configuration.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Check network connectivity before making requests.
PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS Detect when a locked app is in the foreground.
QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES Enumerate installed apps for the lock list.
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW Display the lock screen overlay on top of other apps.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE Run the app monitoring and call blocking service in the background.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Restart monitoring and blocking services after device reboot.
CAMERA / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CAMERA Capture intruder photos on failed unlock attempts (optional, user-enabled only).
USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT Allow biometric unlock of locked apps.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Show foreground service notification and security alerts.
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS Prevent Android from terminating background monitoring service.
READ / WRITE / MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Save wallpaper images and backup files to local storage.
VIBRATE Haptic feedback during PIN/pattern entry.
AD_ID Required by advertising SDKs for ad serving and measurement.
Accessibility Service Detect app foreground transitions to show lock screens reliably.
Device Administrator Optional: prevent uninstallation without the app's password.
READ_CONTACTS / WRITE_CONTACTS Read and restore contacts for the Contact Backup feature. Used only when you initiate a backup or restore operation.
READ_CALL_LOG / WRITE_CALL_LOG Read call history for the Call Log Backup feature. Used only when you initiate a backup or restore.
READ_SMS Read SMS messages for the SMS Backup feature. Used only when you initiate a backup. Messages are stored in a local file on your device.
READ_PHONE_STATE Monitor incoming call state to enable the Call Blocker feature. No call data is transmitted.
ANSWER_PHONE_CALLS Programmatically reject/answer calls on Android 8+ as part of Call Blocking functionality.
04 Advertising

ProLocker is a free application supported by advertisements. We work with the following advertising partners:

  • Tapsell – Native and interstitial ads
  • Adivery – Native ads
  • Google AdMob – Native and interstitial ads (Google Play variant)

These partners may collect your advertising ID, IP address, device information, and interaction data to serve personalized or contextual advertisements. Each partner operates under their own privacy policy:

  • Tapsell Privacy Policy
  • Adivery Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

You can reset your Google Advertising ID at any time in your Android device settings under Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID.

05 Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data — including your contacts, call logs, or SMS content — to any third party. Data may be shared only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Advertising Partners: As described in Section 4, for ad delivery and measurement only.
  • Firebase (Google): For push notifications. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by applicable law or valid governmental request.
06 Data Security

Your lock credentials are stored in encrypted form using Android's secure DataStore. All server communication uses HTTPS. Contact, call log, and SMS backup files are stored on your device in a location you control.

Since all sensitive personal data (contacts, SMS, call logs) never leaves your device, the risk of server-side breaches affecting this data is minimal by design.

07 Data Retention

All data stored by ProLocker is retained on your device until you uninstall the App or manually clear the App's data. Backup files you create are stored in the location you chose and are under your control.

Uninstalling the App removes all App-generated data from the device. Your backup files in external storage will remain until you delete them manually.

08 Children's Privacy

ProLocker is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.

09 Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of data we hold about you.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data.
  • Opt-out: Opt out of personalized advertising by resetting your Advertising ID.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Portability: Your backup files are in standard formats and fully portable.
